Design checks

    Production zones in the shed

    Laid out in process order so profiles move one way: store → cut → weld → assemble → glaze → dispatch. UPVC profiles come in 5.8–6 m lengths, so keep the cutting bay at least 8 m deep and the profile racks along the long wall.

    Roofing options compared

    Each option is priced on your current shed size, with everything else unchanged. The shell cost covers foundations, structural frame, roof and walls. Pick one to make it the active design.

    Detailed estimate (bill of quantities)

    Rate library

    Indicative 2026 rates in Indian rupees, excluding GST, for supply and installation unless stated. Replace them with your local contractor quotes; every estimate line uses these numbers. Edited rates are outlined.

    Quantities come from rule-of-thumb engineering ratios (steel kg/m² by span and height, footing sizes by tributary area, concrete per m² of built-up area). They are good for budgeting and comparing options, not for tendering. Get the structure designed by a licensed structural engineer, confirm setbacks and ground coverage with your local planning authority (DTCP / industrial estate rules), and take three quotes for the big items: PEB package, flooring and roofing.
